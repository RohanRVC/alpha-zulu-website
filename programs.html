Our Programs

Little steps, big flights

Four stages that grow with your child, from first play to school readiness.

🧸

Playgroup

Age 2.5 years+

🎨

Nursery

Age 3 years+
⏰ 9:30 am – 12:00 pm

Our approach

How children learn at Alpha Zulu

🎲

Learn Through Play

Children explore concepts through engaging activities, games, stories and practical experiences.

🔭

Discover Through Curiosity

We encourage children to ask questions, observe their surroundings and learn through exploration.

🎭

Express Through Creativity

Art, music, movement, storytelling and imaginative play help children communicate and develop their ideas.

🌤️

Grow Through Everyday Experiences

Age-appropriate activities support independence, cooperation, decision-making and practical life skills.

💞

Build Social and Emotional Skills

Children recognise feelings, interact with others, practise kindness and develop confidence.
