About Alpha Zulu

Every child is unique

We see every child as an individual with their own interests, strengths and potential.

Who we are

Our vision is to create an early learning environment where children feel safe, valued and encouraged to explore. Meaningful learning happens when children are actively involved, through stories, conversations, creative expression, hands-on activities, movement, play and discovery.

We aim to nurture not only school readiness but also the essential life skills children need as they grow: independence, kindness, confidence, curiosity, communication and cooperation.

As a preschool in Kankavli, we aspire to bring together a progressive approach to early learning with the warmth, care and sense of community that make childhood special.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, curious, compassionate and independent learners who are prepared to embrace new experiences and future possibilities.

Our Mission

To provide a caring, engaging and age-appropriate early learning experience that encourages every child to explore, discover, communicate and develop at their own pace.

What we stand for

Our Core Values

💛 Care

Making every child feel welcomed, respected and valued.

🔍 Curiosity

Encouraging children to ask questions and discover new things.

🌟 Confidence

Giving children opportunities to express themselves and try independently.

🎶 Creativity

Encouraging imagination through art, music, stories and hands-on experiences.

🤝 Kindness

Building empathy, sharing, cooperation and respect.

🌱 Growth

Celebrating individual progress, effort and small achievements.

Meet Our Founder

A Global Journey. A Heart for Little Learners.

With 15 years of professional experience, including a decade in aviation and the privilege of travelling across 20+ countries, my journey has been shaped by diverse cultures, meaningful connections, and a deep understanding of care, safety, discipline, and responsibility.

My aviation experience taught me the importance of nurturing trust, understanding individual needs, and creating a safe and welcoming environment. These values have inspired me to embark on a new journey: building Alpha Zulu – A Nexus International Preschool.

I believe early childhood is not just about learning letters and numbers; it is about nurturing curiosity, confidence, creativity, and kindness. My vision is to create a joyful, safe, and stimulating environment where children learn through play, exploration, nature, and meaningful experiences.

Inspired by my global exposure, I aspire to help little learners develop an open mind, a love for learning, and the confidence to explore the world around them.

At Alpha Zulu, we don't just prepare children for school. We prepare them for life.

From A to Z, We Grow Future Minds.
