FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When will admissions begin?

Admissions are planned to begin in January 2027.

Which programs will be available?

Playgroup, Nursery, Junior KG and Senior KG.

How long will the preschool session be?

Playgroup and Nursery run from 9:30 am to 12:00 pm. Junior KG and Senior KG run from 10:00 am to 12:30 pm.

What age can my child join?

Playgroup: 2.5 years and above. Nursery: 3 years and above. Junior KG: 4 years and above. Senior KG: 5 years and above.

How can I enquire about admission?

Parents can contact the school through the website enquiry form or the school's official contact details.

How can parents learn more about the curriculum and activities?

Contact the school for details about the learning framework, daily routine, age eligibility and available programs.

What makes Alpha Zulu special?

Our vision is to combine caring relationships, joyful exploration, foundational learning and a progressive outlook to create a positive beginning for every child.
